What's on Stix

Everything runs on our own custom plugins.

💰

Farm economy

Build massive kelp, sea pickle, cactus and log farms and sell the output. Live prices show at the top of your screen.

💎

Crystal PvP

Crystals, anchors and totems, with a combat tag so nobody logs out mid-fight.

⚔️

Duels

Kit duels or own-gear duels for keeps on floating ruin arenas. Bet money or shards.

🎁

Crates

Five crates from Common to Amethyst. Pick your prize, then confirm it.

🕷️

Spawners

Loot spawners that fill up over time. Collect or sell the drops right from the menu.

🏛️

Auction house & orders

List items with /ah or post buy orders with /order. Climb /baltop.

✦

Shards

Earn shards in the AFK arena or 10 per player kill. Spend them in /shardshop.

🌆

Megacity spawn

A huge futuristic city with a group RTP zone that sends your whole squad to the same random spot.

🛡️

Fair play

Grim anticheat plus our own checks for macros, auto totem, killaura, x-ray and dupes.

How to join

Free to play on Java and Bedrock.

Java Edition

Multiplayer → Add Server → enter stixsmp.net. Use version 1.21.11.

Bedrock Edition

Servers → Add Server → address bedrock.stixsmp.net, port 41585.

Read the rules

Agree to the rules when you first join, then run /rtp and start your base.
